# LE’Golu

Open `dist/index.html` in Chrome or Edge. Everything, including the original synthesised music, works locally without installation or internet.

For projection, use a 16:9 landscape display. Click Fullscreen and Enable music. Controls fade when the mouse is still. Slides automatically advance every 10 seconds and loop through 20 slides.

The nine pictures are expected in traditional order, left to right as viewed by visitors. In Setup, adjust the first and last picture positions to align the downward arrows with your actual Golu padi. The intermediate positions are evenly spaced. Settings persist in the browser. Disable pointers if the projection and pictures cannot be aligned.

Keyboard: arrow keys navigate; Space pauses; F toggles fullscreen; M toggles music. Setup also accepts an optional local audio recording and has a timing setting. Keep the tab in the foreground for projection. Built-in music uses Web Audio and needs one click to start. A chosen audio file is used only for the current session.

Images remain uncropped. Animated mandalas and drifting lights provide the moving background; reduced-motion system preferences are respected. Story sources are linked in `dist/sources.html`.
